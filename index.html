Common questions

What do I need to send?

A link to your raw footage (Drive, Dropbox or Loom), the platform you post on, and one or two videos whose style you like.

How fast is delivery?

You get the first version within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage and details. Delays on your side move the timeline.

What if I want changes?

Every video includes revision rounds (1 on Starter, 2 on Growth). Extra rounds are charged separately, and I’ll tell you the cost before I start.

How does payment work?

50% upfront when we agree on the plan, and the rest when I deliver. Final files are released after payment. We confirm everything in a short written agreement.
